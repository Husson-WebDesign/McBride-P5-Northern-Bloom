How Event Planning Works

Four steps from first inquiry to final planning.

  1. Inquiry

    The client provides basic information about:

    • Event type
    • Event date
    • Venue
    • Estimated guest count
    • Floral needs
    • Approximate budget

  2. Consultation

    The studio discusses:

    • Style
    • Colors
    • Floral preferences
    • Event priorities
    • Venue requirements
    • Budget

  3. Floral Proposal

    Northern Bloom develops a proposed floral direction and estimated pricing based on the information discussed.

  4. Final Planning

    Details are confirmed closer to the event date. Final quantities, timing, delivery, and installation requirements are reviewed.

Event Policies

What to know before you book.

  • Event dates are not considered reserved until required booking arrangements are completed.
  • Event proposals are based on the information provided by the client.
  • Significant changes in guest count, design, venue, or floral quantities may affect pricing.
  • Delivery and setup charges may vary by location and complexity.
  • Some installations may require approval from the event venue.
  • The client should provide accurate event timing and venue information.
  • Flowers are natural products and may vary slightly in color, shape, and size.

Changes and Cancellations

Floral and event orders often require advance purchasing and preparation. Clients should contact the studio as soon as possible if event plans or floral requirements change.
