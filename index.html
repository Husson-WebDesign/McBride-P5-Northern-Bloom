A floral design studio in Bangor, Maine.

Northern Bloom Floral & Events creates thoughtful floral designs for life's everyday moments and most meaningful celebrations.

Our studio works with individuals, families, businesses, and event clients to create flowers that reflect the purpose and style of each occasion.
