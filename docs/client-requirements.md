## 2. Business Overview

Northern Bloom Floral & Events is a locally operated floral design studio serving customers throughout the Bangor area.

The business creates floral arrangements for everyday occasions, weddings, corporate clients, sympathy needs, seasonal celebrations, and special events.

In addition to individual floral orders, Northern Bloom also provides event floral planning and design services for weddings and private celebrations.

The studio focuses on thoughtful design, seasonal inspiration, professional service, and arrangements that feel personal rather than mass-produced.

## Our Story

Northern Bloom Floral & Events was created to provide floral design that feels thoughtful, seasonal, and personal.

The studio began with everyday arrangements and gradually expanded into wedding and event floral design.

Today, the business serves customers looking for everything from a simple bouquet to coordinated floral design for larger celebrations.

---

## Our Design Approach

The studio believes that floral design should support the mood and purpose of an occasion.

Design decisions may consider:

* color,
* season,
* flower availability,
* venue,
* event style,
* arrangement size,
* budget,
* and client preferences.

Rather than producing identical arrangements, the studio uses available flowers and design principles to create compositions suited to each request.

---

## Seasonal Inspiration

Flowers are naturally affected by season and availability.

Northern Bloom Floral & Events uses seasonal materials whenever appropriate and may recommend alternative flowers when a requested variety is unavailable.

The studio values natural variation and does not expect every arrangement to look exactly identical.

---

## Client Collaboration

For event work, the studio collaborates with clients to understand:

* event type,
* venue,
* guest count,
* color preferences,
* desired atmosphere,
* floral priorities,
* and budget.

The goal is to develop a floral plan that feels coordinated and realistic for the event.

# 18. Frequently Asked Questions

The following questions and answers are approved for website use.

---

### How far in advance should wedding consultations be scheduled?

For weddings and larger events, the studio recommends contacting Northern Bloom approximately **six to twelve months in advance** whenever possible.

Popular dates may book earlier.

---

### Can you recreate an arrangement exactly from a photograph?

Reference photographs are welcome for style inspiration.

However, flowers are natural products and availability changes by season.

The studio cannot guarantee an exact copy of another arrangement.

---

### Do you deliver?

Yes.

Delivery is available for approved orders and events.

Delivery charges depend on:

* location,
* order size,
* timing,
* and setup requirements.

---

### Are flowers seasonal?

Yes.

Certain flower varieties are only available during specific times of the year.

The studio may recommend comparable substitutions when necessary.

---

### Can I choose specific flower colors?

Yes.

Clients may provide preferred colors and general style direction.

Specific flower varieties may still depend on availability.

---

### Do you provide flowers for small events?

Yes.

Northern Bloom works with events of different sizes.

The Essential Package is designed for clients who need a more focused floral plan.

---

### Are consultations required for weddings?

A consultation is strongly recommended for weddings and larger events because floral needs usually involve multiple arrangements, quantities, delivery, and setup considerations.
